# E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

##  Project Overview

The *E-Commerce Sales Analysis Dashboard* is a data analytics project developed using Microsoft Power BI to explore e-commerce sales performance, product categories, customer demographics, shipping costs, discounts, and order-related information.

The project transforms raw e-commerce data into interactive visualizations that help users understand sales patterns, compare product categories, analyze customer behavior, and identify opportunities for business improvement.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze overall e-commerce sales performance.
- Compare sales and pricing across product categories.
- Understand customer demographics, including age and gender.
- Examine product-level sales using product IDs.
- Analyze shipping costs and delivery time.
- Explore discounts and their relationship with product prices.
- Investigate order return information.
- Present business insights through an interactive Power BI report.

## Tools & Technologies

- *Microsoft Power BI* — dashboard development and data visualization
- *Power Query* — data inspection and transformation, where applied
- *DAX* — calculations and measures, where implemented
- *Data Modeling* — organizing data for analysis, where applicable

## Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales & Business Metrics
Analyze key numerical fields, including:
- Total amount
- Product price
- Quantity
- Shipping cost
- Profit margin

These fields provide a foundation for exploring sales performance and related business metrics.

### 2. Category Analysis
Compare product categories using price and discount information to understand differences between categories.

### 3. Customer Demographics
Explore customer age and gender alongside product category and pricing data to identify patterns in customer purchasing behavior.

### 4. Product-Level Analysis
Use product IDs and total amount to compare product-level sales values and identify products for further investigation.

### 5. Delivery & Shipping Analysis
Analyze delivery time in days by product category and examine shipping costs to understand logistics-related patterns.

### 6. Discounts & Returns
Explore discounts across categories and examine order return information to support further investigation of promotional performance and returns.
